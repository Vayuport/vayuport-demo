Our Solutions · 01

Vertiport Infrastructure & Development

Ground infrastructure is where electric air mobility begins. We assess existing helipads, identify and evaluate new sites, and plan and develop helipads, vertiports and hybrid aviation facilities — always subject to technical feasibility and the approvals of the competent authorities.

01

Modification of Existing Helipads into Vertiports

Assessment and transformation of existing helipad infrastructure into facilities designed for potential eVTOL operations, subject to technical feasibility and applicable approvals.

Typical scope

  • Review of the existing helipad, its structure and its surroundings
  • Identification of modifications for potential eVTOL operations — landing area, safety areas, passenger access and charging provisions
  • Implementation of agreed modifications, subject to technical feasibility and applicable approvals

02

Vertiport Conversion Consultancy

Consultancy for evaluating existing helipads and identifying infrastructure modifications required for potential vertiport development.

Typical scope

  • Evaluation of existing helipads for potential vertiport development
  • Identification of the infrastructure modifications required
  • Findings and options to support the owner's decision-making

03

Helipad Site Selection, Construction & Regulatory Consultancy

Site evaluation, infrastructure planning, construction consultancy and coordination of regulatory approval requirements for helipad development.

Typical scope

  • Identification and evaluation of candidate sites
  • Infrastructure planning and construction consultancy
  • Coordination of the documentation needed for regulatory approval requirements
Enquire about this service →
04

Hybrid Helipad Site Selection & Development Consultancy

Consultancy for identifying suitable locations and planning hybrid aviation facilities designed around applicable operational and infrastructure requirements.

Typical scope

  • Screening of locations suited to hybrid aviation facilities
  • Planning for shared use by helicopters and future eVTOL aircraft
  • Design inputs aligned to applicable operational and infrastructure requirements

05

Helipad, Vertiport & Hybrid Aviation Facility Development

Planning, construction and development of aviation ground infrastructure, including helipads, vertiports and hybrid aviation facilities.

Typical scope

  • Development planning and design coordination
  • Construction of helipads, vertiports and hybrid aviation facilities
  • Support through handover towards operational readiness

Regulatory note. VAYUPORT provides planning, consultancy, coordination, development and operational services. Statutory approvals for aviation infrastructure and operations are granted only by the competent authorities. VAYUPORT does not grant aviation permissions or guarantee approvals.
