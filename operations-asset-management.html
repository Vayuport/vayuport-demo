Helipad & Vertiport Operations Management

Operational planning and management services for helipads, vertiports and hybrid aviation facilities.

Typical scope

  • Operational planning and procedures for the facility
  • Day-to-day management of landing area, passenger and ground activities
  • Coordination of on-site safety processes

Aviation Infrastructure Maintenance & Asset Management

Infrastructure maintenance planning, asset management and facility upkeep to support safe and reliable operations.

Typical scope

  • Maintenance planning for pads, lighting, electrical and charging systems
  • Asset registers, condition tracking and lifecycle planning
  • Routine facility upkeep to support safe and reliable operations

Aviation Operations Manpower Deployment

Deployment and coordination of trained personnel for helipad and vertiport operations, subject to required training and qualifications.

  • Coordination and supervision of on-site teams
  • All deployment subject to the required training and qualifications
