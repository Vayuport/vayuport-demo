Applications

Where air mobility infrastructure fits.

From airports and hospitals to hill towns, these are the segments where helipads, vertiports and hybrid facilities could add real connectivity.

  • Airport-Connected Vertiports
  • Hotel & Hospitality Vertiports
  • Hospital & Emergency Aviation Infrastructure
  • Commercial & Mixed-Use Developments
  • Government & Regional Connectivity
  • Remote & Difficult-Terrain Connectivity

Conceptual applications. The segments on this page describe how VAYUPORT infrastructure could potentially be applied. They are not operational projects, and any operations would be subject to the approvals of the competent authorities.

01Conceptual application

Airport-Connected Vertiports

Vertiports located within or close to airports could connect air travellers directly with city centres, business districts and regional towns, without the road transfer that often defines a trip.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Planning around the airport's terminals, ground transport and landside access
  • Hybrid facilities that serve helicopters today and could serve eVTOL aircraft in future
  • Early coordination of airspace requirements around the aerodrome
02Conceptual application

Hotel & Hospitality Vertiports

Rooftop or on-site facilities could give hotels and resorts a direct air link to airports and to destinations that are slow to reach by road — including heritage, nature and eco-tourism locations.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Assessment of rooftop and on-site options
  • Guest-facing passenger areas designed as part of the property
  • Facilities planned so operations can be managed on behalf of the owner
03Conceptual application

Hospital & Emergency Aviation Infrastructure

Helipads and future vertiports at hospitals could support medical transfers and time-critical access, particularly for patients in areas far from specialist care — subject to the applicable operational approvals.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Rooftop and ground-level helipad assessment for hospitals
  • Planning for future eVTOL compatibility
  • Operational readiness and maintenance of the facility
04Conceptual application

Commercial & Mixed-Use Developments

Commercial towers and mixed-use developments could integrate vertiports at roof or podium level. Planning these facilities early, at design stage, is far simpler than retrofitting them later.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Design-stage planning with developers and architects
  • Structural, access and safety-area considerations
  • Long-term operations and asset management
05Conceptual application

Government & Regional Connectivity

A planned network of helipads and vertiports could link district headquarters, regional centres and transport hubs, supporting public services, administration and economic activity across a region.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Network-level site identification and evaluation
  • Phased development, from helipads today to vertiports in future
  • Coordination support with the relevant authorities
06Conceptual application

Remote & Difficult-Terrain Connectivity

In hilly, forested and river-crossed terrain — much of it found across Northeast India — well-sited facilities could shorten journeys that take many hours by road, and support access during disruption.

How VAYUPORT could support

  • Site selection that accounts for terrain and weather exposure
  • Compact, robust facilities suited to remote locations
  • Operations and maintenance planning for limited-access sites
